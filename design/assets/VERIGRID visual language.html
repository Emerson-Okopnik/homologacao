Rules of the system

  • One white slash per composition, echoing the cut in the logo symbol. Never repeat it more than once per frame.
  • Planes use only the navy-to-emerald progression. Transparency and thin lines add depth; never add a new hue.
  • Nodes sit on line intersections. One node per composition may glow, in emerald only.
  • Keep at least 40% of any frame empty and flat for headlines, buttons and interface elements.
  • Do not redraw, rotate or distort the VERIGRID logo. Use it only as supplied.
